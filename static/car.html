Step 1: Why Car Speakers Distort at 60% Volume (& How to Fix Before Testing)

  1. On Pioneer DMH-Z5290BT, set Master Volume to 22–24 (55%–60%) — your real loud driving volume.
  2. CRITICAL Anti-Distortion Settings: Turn Loudness = OFF, Bass Boost = 0, and set SLA (Source Level Adjuster) = -2 dB. (Leaving Loudness or Bass Boost ON adds +10 dB at 100 Hz, which clips the Pioneer amplifier above 45% volume!).

  4. Hold the phone at ear height at the driver's headrest and tap ▶ Calibrate All Car Speakers (Natural Studio Reference).
